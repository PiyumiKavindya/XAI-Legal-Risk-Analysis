Project name and idea explanation.

Explainable AI-Powered Legal Contract Intelligence System for Risk Assessment and Temporal Compliance Monitoring 

**1\. What is Explainable AI**

Explainable AI means that the system should not only identify a risky clause, but also explain why it considers the clause risky and what evidence or regulation supports that decision 

A normal AI system might say:

> ❌ **Risk Level: High**

But the user will ask:

> **"Why is this clause high risk?"**

If the AI cannot explain that, it becomes difficult for a lawyer to trust the system.

Your system should instead provide something like:

> 🔴 **High Risk**

> **Reason:** The clause allows personal data to be shared with third parties without clearly defining consent requirements.

**2.what is Legal Contract Intelligence?**

Contract intelligence means using AI to automatically understand and analyze the contents of a legal contract instead of relying completely on manual review. 

For example:

> "The customer must notify the company within 7 days of any data breach."

A human lawyer may need to manually read the contract and identify important obligations.

Your system will use AI/NLP/LLMs to understand the contract automatically.

It can:

* Extract important clauses  
* Identify obligations  
* Identify parties and responsibilities  
* Detect important dates  
* Identify potentially problematic clauses

**3.What is**  **Risk Assessment**

Risk assessment means identifying potentially problematic contract clauses and classifying their level of legal or compliance risk 

| Risk Level | Meaning |
| ----- | ----- |
| 🟢 Low | Little or no significant risk |
| 🟡 Medium | Potential legal/compliance concern |
| 🔴 High | Significant legal or regulatory risk |

**4.What is Temporal Compliance Monitoring**   
Temporal compliance monitoring means continuously checking whether a contract remains compliant as laws, regulations, and contractual deadlines change over time. 

It can monitor:

> **Contract \+ Regulation \+ Time**

and identify when compliance status changes.

**Flow** 

> **"First, the user uploads a legal contract into the system."**

**1\. Upload Contract**

> "The system first accepts the contract, for example as a PDF or Word document."

**2\. Extract Contract Text**

> "Then, the system extracts the text from the document so that the AI can process and analyze it."

**3\. Understand Contract Clauses**

> "Next, the AI analyzes the contract and identifies the different clauses, obligations, parties, dates, and other important information."

**4\. Identify Risks**

> "After understanding the clauses, the system identifies potentially risky clauses and classifies their risk level, such as low, medium, or high."

**5\. Check Against Regulations**

> "Then, the system checks those clauses against the relevant laws and regulatory requirements to determine whether they are compliant."

**6\. Explain the Risk**

> "This is the Explainable AI part. The system doesn't just say that a clause is risky. It explains *why* it is risky and provides the supporting evidence or relevant regulation."

**7\. Monitor Changes Over Time**

> "The important additional part of my research is temporal monitoring. If a regulation changes in the future, the system can identify that the contract's compliance status may also have changed."

**8\. Generate Compliance & Risk Report**

> "Finally, the system generates a report showing the identified risks, compliance status, explanations, and recommended actions."
